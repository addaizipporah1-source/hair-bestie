# Golden Realm background

`golden-realm-architecture-photo.jpg` is a compressed, camera-photographed architectural interior with gold-lined columns and a central arch. It anchors all four pages, replacing the unrelated salon photographs that were previously used as full-page backgrounds. Page-specific CSS changes the crop, warmth and lighting so the pages feel like connected rooms in one place.

Source: [Elegant Architectural Interior with Golden Accents](https://www.pexels.com/photo/elegant-architectural-interior-with-golden-accents-38993224/) by Rüveyda Akkaya, available under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/).

The prior salon photographs in this folder are retained as unused files. No people or hairstyles are present in the new architectural scene.
